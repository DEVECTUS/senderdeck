# Provider marks

Provider marks are served locally, without contacting third parties when a page loads.

- Google: Google-hosted G asset, https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googleg/1x/googleg_standard_color_128dp.png. Guidance: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
- Microsoft: four-square mark using Microsoft’s standard colours. Guidance: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps.
- OpenAI, Facebook, Instagram, Meta and LinkedIn: SVG geometry from the CC0 Simple Icons 11.15.0 release, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons. Brand colours are applied to the original geometry.

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. Marks identify connection providers; they do not imply endorsement.
