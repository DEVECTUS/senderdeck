## Authorship and reply authority

Version 0.4.21 reads Facebook post.from and Instagram media.owner instead of assigning the selected account as the post author. ownership reports verified, different_asset or unverified, with the provider owner ID and field source. A missing owner is unknown, never manufactured. replyAuthority reports the independent permission verdict. For backwards compatibility, owned retains the conservative legacy reply-authority gate; ownedMeaning explains that it is not proof of authorship. An unavailable Facebook can_comment field is now an explicit unverified reply-authority coverage gap. A different author never receives an owned grant. A verified author does not turn unknown can_comment into permission. Instagram author verification is now required by that legacy gate as well. Sending switches, permissions, Page task and exact approval checks remain intact.

These are specific blockers to claiming Instagram ad-creative discovery; they are not proof that the missing Catherine/Kalan comments are ads, hidden or deleted.
