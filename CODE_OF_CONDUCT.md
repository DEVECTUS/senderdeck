# Community expectations

SenderDeck welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, give specific technical feedback, credit others' work and assume good intent while addressing the impact of a problem.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing private information and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces. Do not post private messages, customer data or credentials as part of a discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions and restrict participation where needed to protect contributors and the project. Concerns about conduct can be reported privately to support@devectus.com.au with "SenderDeck community concern" in the subject. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability or privacy reports.

These expectations apply to project-managed spaces and to people representing the project. They do not imply that maintainers can monitor or control unrelated third-party communities.
