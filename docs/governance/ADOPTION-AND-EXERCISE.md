## Activation checklist

1. Read [the procedure](PUBLIC-AUTHORITY-REQUESTS.md) and have an Australian solicitor review its applicability, obligations, secrecy/recordkeeping limits and proposed response workflow. Establish a reliable urgent legal-referral path.
2. Confirm the primary's business name/title, backup and absence escalation. Verify that `privacy@devectus.com.au` is actually monitored and that urgent cases reach the responsible person. This checklist does not set up monitoring automatically.
3. Choose a business-controlled records location **outside Git**. Restrict access to the primary, appointed backup and necessary reviewers; use MFA, encryption and access history/versioning. Check link sharing, inherited permissions, account recovery and backups. Keep legal advice separately with access directed by counsel. Do not create public links or place records in an automatically AI-indexed shared workspace.
4. Copy the blank register/case template there. Define case IDs and restrict the register too. Confirm another unauthorised account cannot access the folder or its links, using only an account you own or are authorised to test. Record actual permission-check results; an empty CSV in the repo is not a configured secure register.
5. Establish a case-specific retention decision/review process with counsel. No default seven-year or other universal legal period is assumed. Record technical deletion/backup limits and an escalation route for preservation before normal expiry; no legal-hold software exists in SenderDeck.
6. Owner adopts the approved version with an actual effective date. Record changes required by legal review. Explain the staff quick instructions to every person with data access, including the owner/custodian; log acknowledgements. Sending instructions to others needs a separate explicit instruction to send.
7. Conduct the synthetic exercise below in the restricted register, clearly prefixed `EXERCISE`. Use fictional identifiers only and send no real requests/data. Record actor, date, observed outcomes and corrections. This proves use of the procedure, not a genuine legal assessment or an actual authority response.
8. Retain the adoption/permission/training evidence. Assess each Meta checkbox separately against controls actually adopted and usable. A process may exist before a real request arrives, but drafted documents alone do not prove it. Do not change the owner's historical “No disclosures” answer because an exercise exists.

It supplies no verifiable legal instrument. No real authority, customer or number is represented.

## Routine communication drafts (review before use)

**Internal escalation:** “An authority data request has arrived. Restricted case reference: [ID]. Claimed deadline: [time/timezone]. Please review urgently through the approved case location. Do not forward the request or disclose data.”

**Acknowledgement, only after identity/secrecy review:** “We have received your request, reference [reference]. We are reviewing its authority, scope and applicable requirements. Please provide [specific missing information] through [verified channel]. This acknowledgement does not confirm whether DEVECTUS holds the information requested.”

**Scope clarification, through the approved legal route:** “Please identify the legal authority, relevant entity/account, required fields, date range/timezone and applicable deadline. We request clarification of [specific scope issue] so the request can be assessed and any lawful response limited to responsive information.”

These drafts do not challenge an actual order, waive rights or extend any deadline. Do not use them where counsel advises that acknowledgement/contact would be prohibited or inappropriate.
